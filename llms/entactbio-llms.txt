# Entact Bio

> Entact Bio is a preclinical-stage biotechnology company in Watertown, Massachusetts developing Enhancement-Targeting Chimeric (ENTAC) molecules — small molecules that recruit deubiquitinases (DUBs) to remove ubiquitin chains from beneficial proteins in order to extend their half-life, relocate them, or restore their function. It is the inverse of targeted protein degradation. Entact Bio publishes **no API, SDK, developer portal, or machine-readable specification**. ## Science

- [Encompass platform](https://entactbio.com/science/): End-to-end platform integrating biological, chemical, and computational tools to identify disease targets amenable to protein enhancement, select an optimal DUB for a given target, and design ENTACs optimized to each target-DUB pair.
- ENTAC molecules: bifunctional small molecules that bring a deubiquitinase to a target protein, opposing the ubiquitin-proteasome pathway rather than exploiting it. Indication areas named publicly are oncology, autoimmune disease, and rare genetic disorders.
